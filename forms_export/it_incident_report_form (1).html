SECTION 3 — DETAILED INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
Provide a clear, factual, chronological description of what happened. Include what was observed, any error messages, alerts, or anomalies noted:
At approximately 14:30 on 13th August 2026, the dive control workstation (DCV-WS-02) in the dive control van aboard MV Hydro Explorer failed to power on during a routine pre-dive systems check ahead of Job No. HDG-JB-2026-0347. The dive supervisor on watch attempted a standard restart with no response – no power indicator lights, no fan activity, and no display output. A burning odour was briefly noted near the rear of the unit. The dive logging software and topside communications terminal hosted on this workstation became unavailable, preventing automated dive log entries. Backup manual logging procedures were initiated immediately. The IT helpdesk was notified by radio at 14:40.

Immediate / Initial Actions Taken at the Time of Incident:
Dive supervisor switched to manual paper logging and confirmed diver comms remained available via the independent backup radio set. Power to the workstation was isolated at the breaker as a precaution against the noted odour. IT Officer (T. Bakare) was dispatched to the vessel and arrived on site at 15:20. Preliminary inspection confirmed no power draw from the unit's PSU. The workstation was tagged out of service and the incident was logged with the IT helpdesk under ticket ref. HDG-IT-TKT-1142.

Detailed Root Cause / Contributing Factors:
Investigation found the internal power supply unit (PSU) had failed, consistent with the burning odour reported. The unit was over five years old and had shown no prior fault history. Age-related component degradation, likely accelerated by the humid offshore environment in the dive control van, is assessed as the primary contributing factor.
Failure in Management / IT Controls (if any):
No scheduled preventive maintenance or PSU health checks were in place for dive van IT equipment. This has been flagged for inclusion in the vessel IT maintenance schedule.

SECTION 6 — RESOLUTION & CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

Date & Time of Resolution: 14/08/2026  Time: 11:15   Total Downtime: 20 hrs 45 mins
Steps Taken to Resolve the Incident:
IT Officer isolated and removed the failed PSU on 13th August. A replacement PSU was sourced from onshore spares stock and transported to the vessel via crew boat, arriving 14th August 09:30. The replacement PSU was fitted and the workstation powered up successfully. Dive logging software and topside comms terminal were tested and confirmed fully operational by the dive supervisor before sign-off. Manual logs recorded during the outage were transcribed into the system.
